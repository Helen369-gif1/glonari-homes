How a Glonari Home Works

A home, a personal AI and a real business, working together for you.

How It Works

The Four Parts

01

Your Home

The house you live in, with long-term, protected occupancy.

02

Gia™

Your personal AI. She learns your goals, does the repetitive work and tells you where your time is worth spending.

03

Your Glonari Home Business™

A real business connected to your home. It turns savings, opportunities and your participation into economic value.

04

Global Connections®

Glonari's network of merchants, service providers and partners. It is where the value is actually created and paid.

Four parts. One simple experience.

Where the value comes from

The Money Comes From Real Activity. Not From Living in the House.

Your Home Business earns from real commercial relationships, coordinated through Global Connections. Because Glonari works with thousands of homes, it can negotiate deals that no single family could get on its own.

Household savings

Better rates on insurance, utilities, maintenance, services and everyday purchases.

Merchant and service relationships

Commissions and rewards from partners you choose to use.

Referrals

Introducing people to Glonari Homes, or businesses to the network, when it fits.

Your knowledge

Reviews, research, local insight and experience that companies value, shared only with your permission.

Gia finds the opportunity. Your participation creates the value.
